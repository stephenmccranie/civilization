# First-hour path — solo and trusted-group play

Status: ordinary gathering, building and facility use on unclaimed land are agreed, without claim protection. A controller secures a site rather than unlocking gameplay. The path below is a design walkthrough, not a required quest chain or a measured one-hour completion target. No new gameplay is implemented.

## Agreed wilderness baseline

Players may gather, build and use facilities on unclaimed land under normal tool, recipe, energy and regional rules. They do not need a claim or membership to begin. Unclaimed sites have no claim-based protection. Taking possession with a controller adds protection; it does not waive resource or geography requirements.

Ownership exceptions need explicit treatment: the agreed owner-only death-recovery container is not made public merely because it lies outside a claim. Future vehicle/shop rights on unclaimed land still need specification. Do not infer that every separately owned object becomes ownerless when its surrounding land is unclaimed.

Existing structures and occupied wilderness sites raise a separate acquisition question: define whether/how a player can claim another person's unprotected build. No automatic builder priority or demolition is established by the wilderness rule.

## Proposed path

| Step | Player experience | Existing basis | Gap to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Orient using terrain and the map | Calorie reserve starts full; exploration/map rules planned | Spawn location and starting items not selected |
| Obtain food | Gather local food or establish river crops | Recovery foraging and crop prototype exist | Regional implementation and normal wild-food yields; morsels are not the intended staple diet |
| Obtain tools/materials | Collect wood, stone and accessible coal; build without claiming | Ordinary manual work and custom Mineral Coal exist | One-log/one-plank recipe changes, tool costs and strategic-resource audit |
| Choose a site | Inspect land suitability and nearby trading opportunities | Regional roles and shop-map visibility agreed | Actual inspection/map systems, travel costs and available unclaimed sites |
| Establish shelter/work | Build and use a small unprotected site | Ordinary building permitted; custom machines exist | Safe defaults for separately owned facilities outside claims |
| Establish a claim | Craft a controller and supply power | Controller direction agreed | Recipe, claim size, consumption, geometry and takeover calendar |
| Make a first trade | Deliver goods to a funded order or spend coal at a shop | Physical fuel currency and shop direction agreed | Shops, funded demand, transport and actual prices |

This is one route, not a mandated order. A player can trade before claiming, arrive at a friend's shared base, remain itinerant or build an independent homestead. Starting alone must not depend on another player giving permission to perform ordinary work.

## Bootstrap checks

- The first controller can be crafted with unclaimed-land access and Tier 1 inputs. Neither its recipe nor its fuel can require already owning a claim.
- A starter's initial fuel must cover useful protected play, not merely the instant of placement. The controller's meaningful minimum cost and recurring draw remain tuning decisions; no free upkeep is implied.
- Accessible subsistence must work with regional crop limits. Uncooked food and gathering need a credible route until a coal cooking station is affordable.
- One-log/one-plank crafting and 32-item stacks must leave tools, a modest shelter and first production achievable without excessive preliminary hauling.
- Display the actual claim power requirement and remaining powered time before commitment. Zero energy ends protection immediately; tutorials cannot silently grant an exception.
- A funded shop order gives a newcomer a way to earn fuel, but its existence depends on player demand. The empty-server bootstrap must still work through gathering, manual production and extraction, without spawned buyers or money grants.
- Map-visible shops can be distant. Do not assume a newcomer can reach a buyer until travel, terrain and spawn are tested together.
- Group membership grants shared access; it must not be required to avoid an otherwise impossible startup chain.

## Next useful decisions

Choose spawn/onboarding behavior and determine whether players start with no items or a minimal kit. Keep that distinct from free currency or free claim fuel. Then specify basic land-controller size/cost targets against this path, without treating the full hour as a numerical promise.

Open mechanics should become measurable acceptance examples before coding. Test the eventual path solo in a fresh world and in an established world with occupied river frontage; these are different conditions.
