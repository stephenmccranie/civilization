# Civilization — development plan

What is deployed, what is known to be missing, and what gets built next. Agreed rules live in [design_direction.md](design_direction.md); shipped behaviour lives in the system docs indexed by [civilization-mod/README.md](civilization-mod/README.md).

## Now

**Deployed.** The dedicated server and the owner's development instance run the current build (version in `civilization-mod/gradle.properties`). Players install the playtest 0.35.31 revision 1 Prism pack, whose updater follows approved GitHub releases. Clients older than 0.35.31 are refused at the handshake (protocol `14/0.35.31-dev`) until their updater installs it on the next launch. Server-owned features such as the daily temperature cycle, Tab ping and shared crafting tables need no client update.

**World.** A Terrain Diffusion world at scale 4 from the owner's fork, regenerated from the same seed with its river network: every block column is worked out from its position alone, so tile edges leave no walls; rivers are drained from the runoff their climate leaves, flow through lakes and are traced once, by the region holding their upper end, so neighbouring regions meet exactly; a river is as many blocks wide as a real river carrying its water is metres, about 8 to 40 blocks, with a graded long profile, waterfalls where it would cut too deep, a floodplain and sine meanders on flat ground, and where its water steps down a block or falls it spills, the first time the chunk ticks, into a rapid a boat can paddle up or a falling waterfall, and where a cave opens beneath it the water pours in; and the land's large shape rises to use the world's height, with ridges and summits steepened and local slopes kept as the model made them; climate follows the model's real heights, while trees and snow follow the air of the raised ground, so forest stops at a real tree line and an alpine band of grass and rock separates it from the snow that outlasts the summer; ore bands rise and stretch with the same lift as the land, so raised ground holds ore at vanilla's depths below its surface. Climate conditioning runs at 1.5 times the generator's frequency, giving temperature regions roughly 7,000 blocks across, and elevation conditioning at SNR 1.0, so highland is common. Climate-based deposits apply, and Survival is the default game mode. Distant Horizons generates chunks around players as they explore; while nobody is online, full-chunk and Distant Horizons pregeneration continue outward from spawn, pausing as soon as anyone joins.

**Built.** Calories, movement and recovery; the Tier 2 stove and a Creative baking-oven model; regional weather, soil water and crop cycles; finite coal and oil deposits, prospecting, engine-drilled oil wells with the Coal Engine and the three-stage refinery; the manual coal prototype with Survival tools; town workshops, shared parts, graded and refined metal, graded equipment and repairs; half-grid cutting, Builder's Line and controller-fed multiblock guides; claims, whitelists, takeovers, trade counters and the Survey Table; the heat field and comfort; freeform engine boats, Coal Bunker and Cargo Tank; a development-power airship; the physical material foundation with exact stone accounting; wheelbarrows, pallets, offhand carrying, backpacks and supply-fed building; shared crafting tables; the Gladiator Pit; a Creative Colt Paterson; the shared cabinet interface with JEI.

**Known limits.**
- Not yet confirmed by real players: a fresh Prism import and in-launcher pack update, a remote join, the Tab ping view, shared crafting tables and the newer inventory controls in ordinary use, and comfort across the revised daily temperature cycle.
- Ordinary Survival and economy balance, crash recovery, multi-client contention and 200-player load are unverified; automated fixtures do not establish them.
- On the server world, pregeneration completion, elevated-river farming and deposit abundance still need broad-world play, and the river network has been judged only in offline renders. Chunks generated before 6 November 2026 keep forest to their summits and snow-free high ground; the tree line and alpine snow begin where newer chunks meet them, and have been judged only in offline renders. Ore bands follow the raised land only in chunks generated after 7 November 2026; older raised ground keeps vanilla's ore heights. Where two river regions disagree that a small stream exists (2 of 37 crossings over 48 regions round spawn), it ends at the 4,096-block region line; rivers that join meet with no step in the water.
- Hauling: loaded balance, large pallet groups, remote contention and cross-chunk restarts are unverified; backpacks, supply-fed building and the Nearby store panel are untested in ordinary play; chests and barrels filled before their 8-block limit keep that stock until emptied; that limit covers only chests, barrels, chest boats and chest minecarts, so hoppers, droppers, dispensers, crafters, placed shulker boxes and pack animals' chests take building blocks in full stacks, and ender chests still work; a pack animal's open chest is not rechecked against claims; vessel cargo has no dynamic mass. Legacy extra blocks are recovered with `/civilization recoverblocks`.
- The Coal Drill still ships alongside the manual coal prototype, whose swing feel needs review. Its custody is established by automated checks only, and a loaded shovel destroyed as a dropped item loses its load.
- Heat uses an intentionally coarse air-transport approximation; regional sleeping and city-scale load are untested.
- Flight optimisation is deferred: the owner considers about 1,000 blocks/s sufficient for now. Above that, readiness braking and frame stalls persist. Survival nuclear flight is not built.
- The stove, oven and Paterson are Creative-only. The Lacertan is a native model study, not a runtime entity.
- Art masters, editable models and review history live in the local `art/` and `concept_art/` folders, outside git.

## Work queue

Items 1–6 are in priority order; the rest are queued in rough dependency order. Each lists what "done" means.

1. **Art pass to the style sheets.** Redraw every custom item, block face, model atlas and interface texture to the approved [style sheets](design_direction.md#setting-and-material-style), one asset at a time through the `civilization-art` route at 64 px. Done: every item sprite except the Paterson ammunition, which goes with the Paterson model, and the hidden legacy Raw Mineral Blend, which is not redrawn; the thermal, civic, workshop and industrial blocks, fittings, glass pipes and Uranium Ore; the wheelbarrow model; the Kiln, Foundry, Fertilizer Furnace, Smithy, Tannery, Textile Workshop, Oil Derrick, Coal Engine and Hot-Bulb Engine as new native models built in sections. Crude oil and the Street Pavers already fit the style and are kept. Remaining, in order: the other multiblock machines as new native models on the same section system (Fired Heater, Distillation Column, Condenser, Coal Drill, Coal Bunker, Cargo Tank); model atlases (backpack, canisters, pallet, Assembly Bench, coal-mining set, stove, oven and Paterson with its ammunition); the cabinet interface.
   *Done when:* every custom texture is redrawn, verified in its visual scene and published, and neighbouring assets read as one family.
2. **Hauling and local building.** Review loaded wheelbarrow handling and acquisition, and building from a bound backpack, on the server world, then close the 8-block limit's gaps in the other vanilla containers and disable ender storage once [universal enforcement](design_direction.md#storage-carrying-and-hauling) is decided.
   *Done when:* a small home and a modest road or embankment are built with and without the wheelbarrow and compared for time, trips and inventory handling; full targets, competing transfers, lost access, destruction and save/reload each keep exactly one stock balance; nothing scans every frame or forces chunk loading.
3. **Powered and regional loop.** One powered extraction or loading upgrade and a useful loaded river voyage on the scale-4 world: a real ocean → river mouth → inland route, representative reserves by climate, settlement and landing sites, and generation cost.
   *Done when:* handling keeps pace with production on that route, and the evidence settles travel, river elevation and world-lifespan questions in the design.
4. **Manual coal replaces the drill.**
   - Custody: `GameTest` and `Fixture -Name coal` hold exact quantities for two miners on one face, pile, cart and screen; repeated requests; access revocation; full or blocked targets; lost support; falling stock; flowing water; pick, cart and screen destruction; death; chunk unload; restart. Remaining: a rule for the load of a shovel destroyed as a dropped item ([open](design_direction.md#manual-coal-mining)). *Done when* every case keeps exact quantities and presentation can never grant stock.
   - Survival: settle preparation power, hauling and work costs; prove small coal → tools → huge vein → prepared fuel with no Creative supplies; then adapt seam generation and tune yield, labour and route throughput together.
   - Presentation: refine pick, shovel, fracture, piles, cart and preparation machine in first and third person, with Photon and with shaders off; mining and loading audio; a bounded busy-face performance sample. The swing feel needs ordinary-play judgement.
   - Retirement: remove the drill recipe and operation, update JEI, prospecting, tooltips and progression, and apply an explicit legacy-ID and stock recovery policy that preserves saves.
5. **Cooking.** Try the oven in an ordinary kitchen and choose its first baking batch; continue stove play to judge cue timing, carryover, warmth and rewards.
6. **Lacertan in game.** Check Minecraft lighting, readability, full animation clearances and performance before publishing the entity. Encounter rules wait for owner decisions.
7. **Freight docking.** Stationary mooring and engine-driven port-to-port loading on land, then one extraction → loaded voyage → unloading loop. *Done when* disconnection, movement, full targets, lost access, chunk unload and competing transfers conserve stock, fuel cost and capacity are credible, and docking works between real peers. Offshore oil design follows route feasibility and keeps the onshore bootstrap.
8. **Playtest reviews.** Builder's Line length, pace and reach; Paterson cadence, reload and damage, then acquisition; the full first tool → carrier → mining and refinery chain in Survival with peers, including batch, labour and repair economics; Gladiator Pit Survival construction, wager UX, four real clients and crash consistency.
9. **Heat.** Section-local scheduling instead of the whole-field exchange, coherent capacity and energy scales, calibration, dynamic geometry, weather and unload transitions, longer equilibrium. *Done when* conservation, no overshoot, source-off cooling and insulation and ventilation ordering still hold.
10. **Energy in joules and smelt, hammer and quench.** Coal is a 250 MJ scuttle, fuel, machines and claims read in joules, coal machines store heat in their masonry, and every metal is [smelted, hammered and quenched](design_direction.md#workshops-parts-and-equipment); next the [heat field](design_direction.md#heat-shelter-and-comfort) stores joules from the real material data. *Done when* the first iron anvil needs no metal before it, every Foundry metal passes an anvil, metal stays hot in a hot Foundry and cools on time wherever it is, water quenches it at a grade cost that rewards cherry-red timing, diamonds are cut without heat, every hammering spends its labor and raises its units, and the heat field conserves joules within tolerance.
11. **Physical foundation.** Prove per-position mass hooks against the pinned Sable and add dynamic cargo mass and vessel thermal state.
12. **Airships.** Landing and contact coverage with exact hull, cargo and identity through assembly, disassembly and reload; dynamic mass and finite stabilisation; drag and wind calibration on equal-mass hulls; the Survival nuclear loop with exact finite consumption and visible lift margin; real-peer access, reconnects, walking aboard and controller art.
13. **Food and farming audit.** Non-farmland habitats and the food economy, especially berries and supplied animal food.
14. **Ownership, trade and recovery.** A real multi-client protection audit, owner-only death recovery edge cases, inaccessible claims and vehicle custody; external counter storage, item search and destination pins; competing-client transactions.
15. **Operations before public investment.** Production hosting setup; multi-client verification of state, permissions, transfers and reconnects; consistent backups with a demonstrated restore; compact diagnostics for energy, production, transfer failures and performance; a hosting budget with chunk, entity and machine limits; growing-load tests across exploration, hubs, factories, freight, saves and backups; economy evidence from repeated voluntary farm, forest and mine → carrier → buyer exchanges. Public access is staged by that evidence.
16. **Later.** Pickpocketing and the PvP flag; resource-site sieges and the coal control point; maker's marks; husbandry; broader oil and uranium industry, rail and large construction; sky power, regional towers and the Apogee Pyramid; immersion treatment for farming and boats.
