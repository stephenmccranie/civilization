# Frontier uranium art prompts

## Geiger counter and ore block master

Create a high-resolution PIXEL ART concept sheet for two separate Minecraft 32×32 inventory-scale assets in Civilization's alternate 1890s Tesla-era visual world. Show (LEFT) a hand-held Geiger counter as a compact dark cast-steel and aged-brass instrument with a distinct rectangular dial, broad cream needle window, and a short black probe/cable loop; its main silhouette and 2-3 large landmarks must remain clear when reduced to 32 pixels. Show (RIGHT) a rare uranium ore as a full square orthographic Minecraft stone-block face: gray stone with just a few large angular muted green/ochre uranium mineral inclusions, physically embedded, not glowing science fiction. Keep each asset isolated in its own square panel, no typography, no label, no decorative background. High-resolution master composed for tiny final icons: large connected color clusters, hard deliberate pixel edges, restrained upper-left lighting, 12-16 purposeful colors per asset, broad metal/stone texture marks 1-3 final pixels, no micro-rivets, gradients, tiny noise, or neon green. The counter should be practical and repairable, compatible visually with a simple pixel-art prospecting rod and industrial coal equipment.

## Raw uranium master

High-resolution pixel-art master for one Minecraft inventory item: a small hand-recovered piece of RAW URANIUM mineral, one irregular angular chunk, mostly dark muted olive green with several broad dusty ochre-yellow fracture faces and a little gray stone matrix attached. It is a naturally mined ore fragment, not processed fuel, not glowing and not a radioactive warning symbol. Isolated on a true TRANSPARENT background, no shadow, no border, no text. Compose for a final 32×32 sprite with a 26–28 pixel silhouette: 3 or 4 large connected facets, each at least several final pixels, 12–16 hard-edged deliberate colors, crisp upper-left illumination, restrained 1–3 final-pixel stone/mineral marks, no gradients, speckling, microdetail or dithering. Fit with Minecraft/Faithful 32x and Civilization's practical late-19th-century frontier material style.

The first sheet's drawn comparison border is excluded by `export.py`; its counter and ore remain the selected direction. The ore block is intentionally a square opaque face. The raw fragment has a transparent silhouette.
