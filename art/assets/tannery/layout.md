# Tannery assembly

The controller remains at front-center. A one-block-high 3×3 brick plinth supports a half-height oak rim around an open center. Two oak eighth cubes occupy the upper halves of the rear rim cells directly beneath the quarter-beam posts, closing the half-block gap. Each shares its cell with the lower rim slab. The posts support a three-block-wide half-grid crossbar. The small copper fitting in the controller art is visual identification, not a new construction part or fluid mechanic.
