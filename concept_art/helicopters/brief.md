# Helicopter alternatives — concept only

Requested scope: two generated mockups, reviewed for theme and implementable form. No vehicle code, flight specification, recipe or progression change is approved by these pictures.

- **Kestrel:** town-built utility rotorcraft, open lightweight steel frame, timber/canvas cockpit, two separated lateral rotors, skids and compact oil aero engine. Surveyor/mechanic transport rather than a freighter.
- **Meridian:** premium enclosed aerial carriage, ivory enamel/turquoise accent, one coaxial rotor mast, compact fuel-fed electrical drive concept, visible glass cabin and skids. Proposed prestige finish without ornamental gears or lightning.
- Shared language: existing industrial steel/engine craftsmanship; sky material palette from the authoritative design. Small brass bearings, functional copper, readable broad pixel clusters. Reference `art/assets/industrial_steel/texture-review.png` supplies material treatment only.
- Geometry targets: two-block-wide cabin; cuboids, trusses and limited bevels; clear rotor/cabin clearance, supported landing gear, whole vehicle in frame and Minecraft human scale. Dynamic rotor meshes would need explicit native animation and collision design later.
- Review: silhouette, rotor axes/count/clearance, weight/readability, supported parts, meaningful material accents and distinguishability of the alternatives. Generated proportions are illustrative, not engineering measurements.
- Gameplay proposals only: refined fuel and lubricant, personnel/light cargo. Airships retain the bulk-freight role. Power-to-weight balance, controls, customization and Sable integration remain unresolved.
