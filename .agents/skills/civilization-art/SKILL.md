---
name: civilization-art
description: Develop Civilization artwork through a compact specification, actual asset refinement and verified native publication.
---

Use the [asset workflow](../../../tools/modeling/README.md#complete-asset-pipeline): **Define → Build and refine → Verify and publish**. Read the relevant shipped feature reference, [visual direction](../../../design_direction.md#setting-and-material-style), and actual neighboring artwork before designing. New assets use `art.py`; existing records keep their existing backend and provenance.

1. Define purpose, recognizable target and functional constraints briefly. Choose one authoritative editable project or procedural recipe. Inspect references with distinct shape/material/style roles. Resolve routine choices autonomously; ask only for missing decisions that change gameplay or scope.
2. Generate one direction when exploration is useful, using the image-generation skill and actual reference images. Existing directions and routine repairs can proceed directly. Preserve exact prompts/references; competing concepts are opt-in. Inspect generated mechanics and material identity; generation success does not establish acceptance.
3. Block out the whole object, then finish a representative material/joint/signature-feature section. **Build the detail:** for machines, give prominent cast frames, door surrounds, hinges, latches and supports actual depth; author fitted face islands for exposed panels with construction seams, fastener seats and selective edge wear. Give handled, heated and sheltered surfaces different treatments where their use explains it. Reusable material tiles are the base layer; mapping the same tile over the whole machine is unfinished. Preserve quiet areas between readable details, and use texture for small hardware that does not change silhouette. For items, inspect the native sprite export first. Compare the actual section with the target at matching scale, correct specific defects and extend its treatment across the visible assembly. Use explicit 64px density; avoid generic noise and decorative machinery.
4. Follow [native modeling practice](../../../tools/modeling/MODELING.md) for measured geometry, legal native formats, UVs, pivots, clearances and scripted batches. Use [sprite guidance](../../../art/textures/sprite-design.md) for small raster items. Preserve manual edits and unrelated editor tabs. Use ordinary and saw-cut blocks where the agreed construction calls for them; inspect complete isometric plans before implementing layouts.

Prevent overlapping surfaces while authoring: adjacent panels/posts should meet at boundaries rather than share an exposed plane; remove buried duplicate faces or shorten the underlying part beneath a raised fitting. Do not hide z-fighting with brighter trim. Keep painted accents within their own UV island with padding; repeated material crops are intentional reuse, while authored islands must not overwrite each other. Thin frames should have a coherent material treatment, not alternating bright pixel bands. Inspect joints at grazing angles and in moving poses. Windows need actual openings/transparent texels; controls need a visible connection to what they operate.

For custom modeled machines, use recessed panels, raised rims and prominent fastener heads as geometry where their depth should be visible; paint only secondary marks. Make an entire exposed metal face from a continuous master crop at its measured pixel dimensions rather than repeating a one-block tile across it. Preserve material grain without introducing a periodic block grid or replacing the material with flat color. Closed doors must meet a real frame/rebate, and fixed surrounds must close incidental gaps around fireboxes; distinguish intentional vents from missing casing.
5. Inspect native pixels, actual model views and normal gameplay distance. Maintain one current comparison sheet. Compare the target's visible construction detail with the actual asset: identify what became flatter, smoother or disappeared, and restore useful depth/accents unless the user's direction calls for simplification. Reject a machine whose broad exposed faces still read as undifferentiated material fill. Review character, materials, readability and function with specific defects and limits. Keep iterating until blocking defects are resolved. Technical validation, cube counts and favorable review prose cannot substitute for inspected evidence.
6. Prepare/validate exports, preview candidates, then run the relevant hidden Photon/Faithful scene. Judge neighboring family fit, relevant inventory/held views, states, moving clearances and construction/collision. Check costly geometry performance; check Sable placement only for assets claimed to work aboard vessels. Publish after current native and in-game reviews, then update affected authoritative docs and commit completed runtime/code only.

No routine user approval gates. When asked only for concepts or planning, finish at that stage and label it. Earlier failed iterations remain provenance; a requested restart establishes a fresh direction. Scale evidence to complexity and report real limitations. Never accept a flaw merely because repeated generation plateaued: correct or simplify the design within scope.
